# Audio Collector

## Purpose

The Audio Collector monitors AudioContext and media-related events.

---

## Objectives

Provide diagnostics for audio applications and streaming workflows.

---

## Areas of Monitoring

### AudioContext

Track:

- creation
- suspend
- resume
- close

### Media Devices

Track:

- microphone availability
- permission state

### Streaming

Monitor audio processing pipelines.

---

## Planned Metrics

- context state
- audio latency
- streaming status

---

## Future Enhancements

- waveform diagnostics
- audio pipeline tracing
- media device reports
